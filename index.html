The Artists

Two Crafts, One Voice

Words

Antonio Santana

Antonio Santana is a Marine Corps veteran and former police officer whose poetry is forged in the space between what he's carried and what he's chosen to become. Writing began as a way to process what words rarely reach — service, struggle, and the daily decision to keep going when everything says otherwise.

A husband and father, Antonio writes with his family as both anchor and audience — every poem shaped by the belief that vulnerability is its own kind of strength. Where Alex builds the sound, Antonio brings the words — raw, lived-in, and unflinching.

Sound

Alex Macias

Driven by a passion for creating unforgettable tracks, Alex Macias is an up-and-coming music producer from San Antonio, Texas, known for blending infectious melodies, hard-hitting drums, and modern production with timeless musical influences.

Drawing from hip-hop, R&B, pop, and cinematic soundscapes, Alex takes Antonio's words and breathes life into them — building melodies, rhythms, and soundscapes that amplify every message.

How It Started

One Conversation

It wasn't planned. Alex was over at the house, and the conversation drifted to music — what we liked, what we were into. Antonio knew Alex made music, but not really what he did with it. So he shared a poem. And the idea for the audiobook he'd been carrying around.

Alex's response was simple: I love this. Let me put music to it.

The first draft he sent back changed everything. Antonio fell in love with what Alex could do with his words — and the two of them started working together in earnest.

What they found, working side by side, is that their stories come from completely different circumstances — but the underlying feeling is the same. Pain doesn't care where it came from. Hurt is hurt. And that's exactly why the collaboration works: Alex comes at it from a different place than Antonio does, but somehow it's still the same place.
